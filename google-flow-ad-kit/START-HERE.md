# Night Economy — Google Flow 15-second ad kit

This kit contains the real product footage and the prompts to build a short, cinematic YouTube advert.

## Files

- `media/current-app-captures/night-economy-tv-live-screen-2026-11-17.mp4` — fresh 10-second, 1920×1080 recording of the deployed TV view, captured 17 Nov 2026. Use a clean 4–5 second section in the final edit. Insert this clip untouched so current prices and text stay accurate.
- `media/current-app-captures/night-economy-tv-1920x1080.png` — current TV view at 1080p.
- `media/current-app-captures/night-economy-market-crash-screen-2026-11-17.png` — current full-screen Beer Market Crash preview with three temporary offer prices.
- `media/current-app-captures/night-economy-portal-1440x900.png` — current venue portal view.
- `media/current-app-captures/night-economy-mobile-430x932.png` — current mobile menu view in portrait format.
- `media/night-economy-brand-board.png` — brand palette and logo reference. Use it for colour and mood only; the final brand mark should be added in the edit, not regenerated by AI.
- `PROMPTS.md` — master creative brief, Flow agent prompt, shot prompts, voiceover and edit timing.

## Recommended Flow workflow

1. Create a new Flow project called `Night Economy — 15s launch ad`.
2. Upload the current app screenshots and TV recording plus the brand board from `media/` to the project. The TV recording is genuine product footage; the screenshots show the current TV, market-crash, portal and mobile designs.
3. Use the **Flow Agent prompt** in `PROMPTS.md` to make a storyboard and create the cinematic pub shots. Generate shots separately so you can keep the real app footage untouched.
4. For each generated scene, select **Video**, use **Ingredients/References** if available, and reference the brand board for palette. Use a consistent cast description in every prompt. Screenshots are visual references for the real interface; do not ask Flow to recreate the screens.
5. Add `night-economy-tv-live-screen-2026-11-17.mp4` directly to the sequence as the live-board product insert. Cut to `night-economy-market-crash-screen-2026-11-17.png` for the crash payoff. Do not run either through generative video editing; AI can distort prices, letters and app details. A subtle editorial push-in on the still is fine.
6. Assemble and trim the clips in SceneBuilder or a video editor to exactly 15 seconds. Add the real logo, captions, voiceover and licensed music during editing.
7. Export 16:9 at 1080p if your Flow plan and export options allow it. Keep text and logo inside the central safe area so it also crops well for social cutdowns.

Flow’s menus, model names, durations and credits change. If a named option differs in your account, choose the closest Video + reference/ingredients workflow and generate 4–6 second shots.

Capture note: the screenshots and TV screen recording in `media/current-app-captures/` were freshly captured from the production site on 17 Nov 2026. They replace the older market-loop footage that was in the first draft of this kit.
