# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Night Economy to be welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive and respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy, venue data, and confidential operational information.
- Report security issues privately rather than publishing exploitable details.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or sharing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as privately as practical. Contributors who breach this code may have their participation limited or removed.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
